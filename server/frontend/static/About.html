About Us

Welcome to Best Cars dealership, home to the best cars in North America. We deal in selling domestic and imported cars at reasonable prices.

Founded in 1998 as a single lot in Austin, Texas, Best Cars now runs a network of dealerships across the United States and Canada. Every vehicle we list goes through a 150-point inspection before it reaches the floor, and each one comes with a full service history so you know exactly what you are buying.

We believe buying a car should be straightforward. Our prices are published up front, our finance options are explained in plain language, and our advisors are paid a salary rather than a commission — so the only thing they are trying to sell you is the right car.
